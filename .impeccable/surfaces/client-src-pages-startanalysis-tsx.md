## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. The "Start Analysis" route: a company-name intake that triggers an AI-driven DISCOVER/REASON/DECIDE research pass (via a conclave session with a new web-search tool, still being built separately) and produces one Opportunity per identified AI use case for that company, with Evidence entries attached. Results view is a live-updating instrument panel that later folds into the existing Portfolio.

## Audience, job, action/task, proof/content, constraints

Internal AI-delivery consultant, not a public/self-serve visitor. Job: build credible groundwork for a client discovery conversation without manual data entry. Action: type a company name, run analysis, review results before/during a client meeting. Proof/content: every AI-generated claim must show its evidence inline — no claim without a visible source. Constraint: search-tool response shape and timing not yet finalized (blocked on Council-of-ai-experts search integration) — build the UI against a stable placeholder data contract for now.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

**Night-Flight Instrument Six-Pack** (locked via Impeccable concept-seed, key f2a4399a, bolder register round 1, winning challenger `signals-instruments-night-flight-six-pack`, beating its own dealt leader `generative-parametric-identity` on both audience identification and product clarity).

World: an analyst's cockpit instrument panel, not a dashboard. Six luminous gauges (Evidence Strength, Source Coverage, AI Confidence, Risk, Feasibility, Value) hold the consultant's trust in the AI output the way a pilot's six-pack holds trust through cloud. Matte black panel ground, luminous white/radium-green markings, amber/red reserved only for genuine caution states — never decorative. IBM Plex Mono for instrument labels/readouts, a plain humanist sans (IBM Plex Sans or DM Sans) for claim prose.

Memorable moment: before running, every gauge reads NO DATA, parked dark. On submit, needles sweep into position live as findings stream in and settle with damped, physical motion (never a generic spinner) — a consultant reads "the panel settling" the way a pilot reads a cross-check completing.

Raised elements to carry into the build:
- From the displaced generative-identity leader: needle positions are literally driven by live confidence data as it streams in — the visualization IS the evidence signal, not a decorative flourish.
- From the competitive iridescent-cloud-edge challenger: an absent/forming/vivid state vocabulary — zero evidence = NO DATA (parked needle), gathering evidence = needle deflecting, strong/corroborated evidence = needle locked solid.

Signature interaction: hovering/focusing any gauge reveals its trend (strengthened or weakened since the last research pass) — a functional cross-check, not a static readout.

Cross-surface reach: the same six-gauge grammar extends later to Experiment status (result/success as two more readings) and to individual Evidence entries (source reliability, recency, corroboration as micro-instruments).

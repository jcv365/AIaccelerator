Company analysis journey: mockups for approval

Nothing is built until you approve these. The app screens use the product's real look (dark navy, blue and multi-accent). The documents use a light page so they read and print well. Names and counts come from the real Maersk run; italic or bracketed text is where researched content will go.

The journey (in the app)

1 · After sign-in: company intakeOne box for a company name or website, plus what we look at. This replaces the dashboard as the first screen. 2 · Analysis in progressStages, elapsed time, and a clear note that you can leave and come back. 3 · Results and downloadsFour downloads (C-level deck and proposal, technical deck and proposal), an honest evidence note, and a regenerate button.
